Hey! I'm Lana, a fourth-year Software Engineering student at the Faculty of Information Technologies in Mostar.

I've always liked creating things, figuring out how stuff works, and occasionally spending way too much time making small details just right. I'm definitely a perfectionist (sometimes more than I'd like to admit), but I guess that's part of the fun.

I got into programming because I liked the idea of being able to build something from scratch and actually see an idea come to life. Since then, I've explored quite a few areas, from web and mobile development to machine learning and IoT. I still have a long list of things I want to learn, and honestly, I don't think that list is ever going to get shorter. There's always something new to try, a different approach to explore, or a skill I want to get better at.
